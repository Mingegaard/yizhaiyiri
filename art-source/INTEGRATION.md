## Implementation

- `src/content/assets.ts`: source model transforms, named model roots, and room furnishings.
- `src/scene/studyLayout.ts`: `STUDY_FURNISHED` is currently false at the user's request. The courtyard variant excludes the complete `study_furniture` group (writing desk, both stools, bookcases, scroll stand, books, paper and tabletop objects), and the separate tally vessel/sticks are not mounted. Their collision volumes and proximity prompts are disabled together. The study's architecture, attached lanterns, exterior planters and furnishings elsewhere remain. Existing records and story progress are retained; activity-based focus/meditation continues through the daily note.
- `src/scene/daybedMeditation.ts`, `src/components/FocusRetreat.tsx`, `src/App.tsx`: approaching the daybed offers a quiet floating meditation button, independently of the removed original desk interactions. The dialog accepts whole-minute durations from 1 to 120, with 5/10/20/30 minute presets; demo mode also exposes an explicit 10-second experience. Entry locks walking, saves the walking refs and eases position plus quaternion into the south-facing axial courtyard view over 2.8 seconds. The persistent timer is created only after the camera reports arrival. Cancelling entry creates no session; early exit and natural completion restore the untouched walking pose. Pause/resume remain available, with a direct visible exit button. Portrait framing includes the front flowering tree and the gate; perspective/FOV restore on exit.
- `src/game/timerDuration.ts`, `src/game/types.ts`, `src/game/engine.ts`: optional `retreat: 'daybed'` metadata identifies saved custom meditation sessions. The selected duration survives normal-mode dispatch instead of being overwritten by the scheduled-task default. Existing focus/meditation activities retain their original durations. Completed daybed sessions are recorded as meditation memories with their actual duration; unfinished ones receive no completion award. Existing version-1 saves remain compatible.
- `src/scene/atmosphereMotion.ts`, `src/scene/CourtyardSky.tsx`, `src/scene/ModelAsset.tsx`: sky drift and foliage clocks advance only for an unpaused seated countdown, staying frozen while walking, during entry, when paused, and after exit. The painted cloud field rotates around the north–south axis at 0.014 radians per second (about 0.80 degrees per second), rising on the left and arcing clockwise to the right in the south-facing daybed view, matching the user's curved arrow. The horizon gradient stays level. The sky retains its last phase without snapping back; reduced-motion preferences use 30% of the drift rate. Each loaded model owns its foliage time uniform without per-frame React updates.
- `src/scene/meditationPetals.ts`, `src/scene/GardenAtmosphere.tsx`: daybed sessions recycle 40 small ivory/blush petals under the front flowering tree in the south courtyard view. Individual 17–23 second falls are staggered continuously from the beginning, with gentle sideways drift and rotation. Paused sessions preserve their positions; outside the seated session the particle pool is hidden. Other activity focus sessions retain their earlier sparse batch timing.
- `src/scene/ModelAsset.tsx`: shared GLB cache, selective architecture, material cloning, crease outlines, night palette, and subtle foliage movement.
- `src/scene/studyScreens.ts`: the user-requested open south study facade. Four source meshes merge all screen leaves by material; cached index filtering removes only the south fixed/folded leaves in the courtyard variant, before ink generation. North/side screens and the reference GLB remain intact. Navigation opens both flanking bays and retains column collisions.

The remaining paper lattice and side-window frames extend upward to the raised beams, keeping lower solid leaves and sill walls fixed; door transom bands rise while thresholds stay at floor level.
